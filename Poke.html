About Pokemon

Pokémon is a Japanese franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri and developed by Game Freak in collaboration with Nintendo. It began in 1996 with Pokémon Red and Green for the Game Boy in Japan , inspired by Tajiri’s childhood hobby of collecting insects. The core idea was to capture, train, and trade creatures called Pokémon, encouraging exploration and player interaction through the link cable.
The success was immediate and soon expanded into the anime starring Ash Ketchum and his partner Pikachu, which boosted its international popularity. By the late 1990s, Pokémon had become a cultural phenomenon with trading cards, movies, and extensive merchandise. Decades later, titles like Pokémon GO revitalized the franchise by integrating augmented reality and connecting millions of players worldwide. Today, it remains one of the most successful and recognizable entertainment franchises globally.

The Games

Since the late 1990s, Pokémon established itself as one of the most influential handheld video game franchises in the world. After its successful debut on Game Boy, the series made the leap to the Game Boy Color (GBC) and later to the Game Boy Advance (GBA), expanding its world, improving graphics, and introducing new generations of Pokémon.

Pokémon Red and Green – 1996 – Game Boy

Pokémon Red for the Game Boy, released in 1996 in Japan, was one of the first Pokémon games created by Satoshi Tajiri. Players take on the role of a young trainer on a journey through the Kanto region to catch and train Pokémon, defeat Gym Leaders, and ultimately challenge the Elite Four to become the Pokémon Champion. Its innovative mechanics, social trading via the link cable, and engaging adventure made it a massive success, launching the Pokémon franchise into a global phenomenon.

The main three

Charmander

Charmander is a Fire-type Pokémon and one of the original starter Pokémon from the Kanto region. It resembles a small, orange lizard with a flame burning at the tip of its tail, which reflects its life force—if the flame goes out, it’s said Charmander would faint.
In the games, Charmander’s main abilities include:
  1. Ember: a basic Fire-type attack that can also burn opponents.
  2. Scratch: a simple physical move.
  3. Flamethrower (learned later): a powerful Fire-type attack with a chance to burn.

Bulbasaur

Bulbasaur is a Grass/Poison-type Pokémon and one of the original starter Pokémon from the Kanto region. It resembles a small, blue-green dinosaur with a plant bulb on its back, which grows as Bulbasaur gains experience.
In the games, Bulbasaur’s main abilities include:
  1. Vine Whip : a basic Grass-type attack that strikes the opponent.
  2. Tackle : a simple physical move.
  3. Razor Leaf : a more powerful Grass-type attack with high critical-hit potential.

Squirtle

Squirtle is a Water-type Pokémon and one of the original starter Pokémon from the Kanto region. It resembles a small, blue turtle with a curled tail and a hard shell that it can retreat into for protection.
In the games, Squirtle’s main abilities include:
  1. Water Gun : a basic Water-type attack that shoots a jet of water at the opponent.
  2. Tackle : a simple physical move.
  3. Bubble : a Water-type move that can lower the opponent’s speed.
